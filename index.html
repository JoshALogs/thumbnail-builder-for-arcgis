Make a thumbnail

  1. Type the title, then pick a layout and a theme.
  2. Add a background photo or a logo if you have one. Without a photo, the theme draws a contour pattern.
  3. Download the PNG. On the item or group page in ArcGIS, choose Edit thumbnail and upload it.

Accessibility

The contrast check measures the text against the actual pixels behind it, using the WCAG 2.1 contrast formula. Thumbnails are usually shown small, so aim for at least 4.5:1.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Images you upload stay on your device. Image URLs and theme packs are fetched directly from the sites that host them. Share links carry only your settings.
